Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our lawn care and snow shoveling services

How quickly do you shovel after a snowfall?

Because we live right here in Williamsburg, we head out as soon as snow accumulates—usually within a few hours of a snowfall stopping (after school on weekdays).

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept both Interac e-Transfer and cash once the job is completed to your satisfaction.

Do I need to be home when you come by?

No! As long as gates are unlocked for backyard mowing or cars are moved if needed, we can complete the work and text you a photo when we're finished.
